Since 2013

We build players, not just courts

Dot M Futsal started with one floodlit pitch and a stubborn belief — that recreational futsal deserves elite infrastructure.

Our story

From one pitch to a full academy

In 2013 our founders — two ex-state footballers and a local entrepreneur — rented a 2,000 sq ft shed and laid synthetic turf by hand. Word spread fast. Within a year, the Sunday league had 16 teams and a waiting list.

Today Dot M Futsal spans three FIFA-spec courts, a coaching academy for 200+ young players, a strength & recovery zone, a cafe and a broadcast corner used by two regional leagues. What has not changed is the intent: every player should leave the building better than they arrived.

The name Dot M comes from the old futsal habit of the dot M pass — a firm, deliberate touch that beats a hopeful one. Small detail. Big difference.

Values

What we refuse to compromise on

Quality of surface

We re-brush the turf daily and replace it every 4 years. No shortcuts, ever.

Fair access

Everyone plays on the same surface, in the same league, with the same referee standard.

Player development

Every player gets a skill plan, reviewed every 8 weeks by a certified coach.

Community first

Free community hours every Sunday morning for schools and grassroots clubs.
