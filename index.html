Why Dot M

Built for real futsal, not five-a-side

Futsal demands sharper passing, faster transitions and constant decision making. Our surfaces, lighting and coaching are engineered for exactly that.

FIFA-spec surfaces

Low-pile synthetic turf with ball-roll certification, so your first touch feels exactly like match conditions.

Full LED floodlights

6000K lighting with zero glare — no more shadow battles while you shoot. Play until 11 PM, every night.

Pro goal & gear room

FIFA-match goals, free goalie gloves and bibs on every court. Arrive with nothing, play like a pro.

Changing & recovery

Hot showers, lockers, a physio corner and an iced recovery zone to keep your legs fresh for the next game.

FAQ

Quick questions

Do I need to bring my own futsal ball?

No. Match-quality futsal balls, bibs and cones are provided on every court. You are welcome to bring your own boots and a water bottle.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Free cancellation up to 12 hours before your slot, and a free reschedule once per booking. Late cancellations are charged 50% — just call us and we will sort it out.

Is parking available?

Yes — 40 free two-wheeler spots and 15 car bays on site, plus paid parking on the service road behind the complex.

Can I host a corporate or birthday event?

Absolutely. We host corporate futsal leagues, birthday battles and team-building events for up to 80 players, with catering options. Send us an enquiry and we will build a package.

Ready to step onto the pitch?

Slots fill fastest on weekday evenings 7–9 PM and every Sunday morning. Book online now, or call us — we always keep a small buffer of walk-in slots at the front desk.
