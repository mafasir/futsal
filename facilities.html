House rules

Keep the game flowing

Good facilities only work if everyone respects a few simple rules. These are posted at every court and enforced by our staff.

  • Only indoor, non-defective futsal shoes on court — no spikes, no football boots with studs, no gum soles.
  • Arrive at least 10 minutes early. If the previous slot overruns, the next team loses warm-up time.
  • Maximum 3 hours per booking so every team gets fair rotation.
  • Kids under 12 must have an adult on the court at all times.
  • No food or drinks on the playing surface — the cafe is right outside.
  • Report any wet patch, torn turf or damaged net to the front desk immediately.
