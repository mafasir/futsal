Wednesday Blitz Nights

Fast, loud, and over before 10 PM

Every Wednesday we convert Court 3 into a 3x3 blitz arena. Eight teams, 12-minute matches, rolling clock, and a running scoreboard on the big screen. It is the cheapest competitive futsal in the city — and the most fun.

  • Entry: ₹900 per team of 6
  • Doors 7:30 PM · First match 8:00 PM
  • Group stage then single-elimination final
  • Weekly prize of ₹5,000 plus trophy shelf bragging rights
  • Open to all levels — mixed and graded categories
