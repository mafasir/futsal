Payment FAQ

Before you pay

Which payment methods do you accept?

UPI, all major credit and debit cards, net banking, and cash at the front desk. Online bookings can be paid on arrival or in advance.

Is there a refund policy?

Free cancellation up to 12 hours before your slot for a full refund. Within 12 hours, 50% is refunded as credit valid for 30 days. No-shows are non-refundable.

Do you offer corporate memberships?

Yes — prepaid corporate accounts from 40 hours a month with a single monthly invoice, plus free venue slots for six team-building events a year. Contact us for a quote.

Are there any extra charges for equipment?

No. Match balls, bibs, cones, goals and even keeper gloves are included in every court rate. Only bounce-house and external catering, if requested, are charged extra.

Can I pay in instalments for academy programs?

Yes — academy and coaching fees can be split into two payments, with the second due before the mid-point of the program cycle. Just ask at the front desk.
