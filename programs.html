The method

How a 12-week block works

Our curriculum is not a random playlist of drills. Each block follows the same four-phase loop so progress is measurable.

1

Weeks 1–3 · Ball mastery

Both feet, first touch, weight transfer and the rondo. Comfort before intensity.

2

Weeks 4–6 · Positional play

Shape, width, depth and rest defence — plus set-piece patterns your team can repeat.

3

Weeks 7–9 · Transition & finishing

Fast breaks, counter-press, finishing from every angle and goalkeeper duel work.

4

Weeks 10–12 · Match IQ

Scrimmages, video analysis, decision-speed drills and a final assessment report.
