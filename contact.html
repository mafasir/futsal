FAQ

Quick answers

What is the fastest way to book a court?

Use the online booking form — it shows live availability, calculates the price instantly and gives you a confirmation reference straight away. For same-day changes, calling is faster.

Do you allow private events after hours?

Yes. We can stay open past 11 PM for birthday parties and corporate events at an additional hourly rate. Send an enquiry and we will quote the full package.

Can I hire the academy coaches independently?

Yes — Level 1 coaches are ₹400/hour and Level 2 coaches ₹700/hour when added to any court booking, minimum 1 hour. For longer coaching blocks, the monthly programs work out cheaper.

I have a complaint about a booking or a facility issue.

We are sorry about that. Email us with your booking reference and we will respond the same working day, and credit your account where the fault is ours.
